// Body Content

Readable Text

Body text is rendered in #E0E0E0 (light grey) for optimal legibility against dark backgrounds. The Inter typeface provides excellent readability at smaller sizes while maintaining the clean, geometric aesthetic of the system.

Secondary text uses a muted grey #A0A0A0 for supporting information that shouldn't compete with primary content for attention.
